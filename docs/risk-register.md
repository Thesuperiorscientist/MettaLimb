# Risk register

Risks are placed on a likelihood and impact matrix, each with a mitigation.

| Risk | Matrix position | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Filament quantity | Mitigate (high likelihood, high impact) | See filament quality below and the PET supply row |
| Funding | Mitigate (high likelihood, high impact) | Phased funding, partnerships |
| PET supply | Monitor (high likelihood, lower impact) | Diversify collection networks, keep buffer stock |
| Beneficiary adoption | Monitor (high likelihood, lower impact) | Community engagement and education |
| Prosthetic fitting | Manage (lower likelihood, high impact) | Training programmes, custom fitting |
| Filament quality | Listed with a mitigation, not placed on the matrix | Quality control protocols and testing |

Quadrant meaning:

| | Lower impact | High impact |
|---|---|---|
| **High likelihood** | Monitor | Mitigate |
| **Lower likelihood** | Accept | Manage |

No risk is currently placed in the Accept quadrant.

Proactive risk management supports sustainable impact. Review this register at each project phase.
