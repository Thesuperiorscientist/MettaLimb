# Open questions on the models

Checks made while turning the pitch deck into code. Resolve them (in the deck and here), then trim or delete this file.

## Numbers that do not reconcile
1. **CO₂ term in the Total Impact Score.** The TIS uses 1,250 as its second term, but 500 tons of PET at λ = 1.5 t CO₂ per ton gives 750. With 750 the score is about 425 instead of 575.
2. **Yield coefficient.** φ = 1,000 units per ton would turn 500 tons of PET into 500,000 units, not 500 prosthetics. Year-1 revenue (125,000) equals 500 devices at $250, so 500 devices anchors the financials. At φ = 1,000 that is about 0.5 tons of PET (500 kg), and the CO₂ and jobs outcomes shrink accordingly. Either the tonnage, φ, or the definition of a "unit" needs fixing.
3. **CPPI normalisation.** The formula divides each component by its maximum across countries, but in the Myanmar table the normalised values for Need (0.076), Readiness (0.740) and Gap (0.215) are just the raw percentages divided by 100. Only Waste looks max-normalised (0.177, implying a maximum of about 8.14 million tons). Using the stated formula for all four components would change the scores. Publish all raw inputs for every country.
4. **CPPI rounding.** The exact Myanmar score from the table is 26.31, which the deck shows as 26.4 because it adds the rounded contributions (0.264).

## Wording and labels to check
5. **Disability figure (Myanmar).** About 2.5 million people with physical disabilities is attributed in the deck to landmine casualties and the civil war. Landmine casualty counts are normally far smaller, so this figure probably refers to physical disability in general. Check it against the source.
6. **Geography.** The OECD Regional Plastics Outlook covers Southeast and East Asia, while the deck labels the 113 Mt and 242 Mt totals as ASEAN. The 2050 leakage figure is written "7..10 Mt"; +103% on 3.5 Mt implies about 7.1 Mt.
7. **Timor-Leste.** It is named among the highest-risk countries but is not in the ten-country CPPI ranking.
8. **Risk matrix.** The matrix lists "Filament Quantity" while the mitigation list says "Filament Quality".
9. **Financial table.** The last column is labelled "Year 2+", probably meant as Year 3+. Currency is a dollar sign on the impact slide and "standardised units" on the finance slides.
10. **Scale elasticity.** ε = 0.6 is called a "cost reduction" but is applied as a multiplier ($250 × 0.6 = $150), which is a 40% reduction.
11. **Mixed units in the TIS.** The score adds devices, tons of CO₂ and jobs with weights. The result depends heavily on the units chosen. Normalising each outcome first would make the weights meaningful.
