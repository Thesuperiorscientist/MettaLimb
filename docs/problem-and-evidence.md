# Problem and evidence

Figures are as presented in the project deck, with the sources it cites. Check each against its source before reusing it elsewhere.

## Plastic waste
| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Global plastic production grew from 2 million tonnes (1950) to more than 450 million tonnes a year | As presented in the deck |
| ASEAN generated 23.17 million tons of plastic waste in 2020, roughly 15,000 Olympic swimming pools when compacted | Our World in Data (2020), OECD (2025) |
| Myanmar has 83% mismanagement risk, the highest in ASEAN, with most waste leaking into the environment | Our World in Data (2020), OECD (2025) |
| Indonesia, the Philippines, Vietnam and Thailand generate 79% of ASEAN's plastic waste | Our World in Data (2020) |
| Myanmar, Timor-Leste, Cambodia and Laos face the highest mismanagement risk | Our World in Data (2020), OECD (2025) |

### Trajectory to 2050 (OECD Regional Plastics Outlook, 2025)
| Measure | 2022 | 2050 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total plastic waste | 113 Mt | 242 Mt | +114% |
| Plastic leakage | 3.5 Mt | about 7.1 Mt | +103% |

## Limb loss and prosthetic need
| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| More than 35 million people live with limb loss worldwide | Worldmetrics, Limb Loss Statistics (Aug 2026) |
| About 1.6 million adults experience a new limb loss each year | Worldmetrics (Aug 2026) |
| Limb loss is concentrated among adults aged 65+, and men are the larger share | Worldmetrics (Aug 2026) |

## Economic burden
| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| About $50 billion a year estimated global economic cost of limb loss | Worldmetrics (Aug 2026) |
| About $8 billion a year in direct medical costs in the US | Worldmetrics (Aug 2026) |
| About $2 billion a year on prosthetics and orthotics in the US | Worldmetrics (Aug 2026) |
| About $50,000 average annual medical cost per amputee in the US | Worldmetrics (Aug 2026) |

## Access gap
| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| More than 2.5 billion people need assistive technology | WHO |
| Access can be as low as 3% in some low-income countries | WHO |
| 15.6% of people in the WHO South-East Asia Region have severe disability | WHO South-East Asia Region |
| Main barriers: cost, limited availability, inadequate financing, shortage of trained personnel | WHO / UNICEF Global Report on Assistive Technology (2022) |
| Around 2.5 million people in Myanmar are estimated to live with physical disabilities; the deck links this to landmine casualties and the civil war, and notes children are about a third of landmine victims | ICRC (2022), Landmine Monitor (2025) |

The Myanmar disability figure should be checked against its source wording. See [model-notes.md](model-notes.md).

- **SDG 10, Reduced inequalities:** support inclusion and accessibility for people with disabilities
